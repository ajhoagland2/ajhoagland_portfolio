Additional Improvement Project

WAT GRID BRACKET

Fixture Standardization

FROM SKILL-DEPENDENT SETUP TO REPEATABLE ASSEMBLY

Problem

Operators assembling mosaics experienced grid movement during tile placement, incorrect grid orientation, and accumulation of tile fragments beneath work surfaces.

Investigation

Operator workflow observations identified three root causes: insufficient grid constraint, lack of orientation control, and debris accumulation affecting seating and visibility.

Solution

Developed a modular fixture system featuring positive grid location, integrated orientation keying, and open-bottom debris evacuation channels.

Result

The fixture transformed the WAT station from a skill-dependent process into a standardized and repeatable assembly system.
