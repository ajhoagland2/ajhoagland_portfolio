Professional Overview

ABOUT

I am a systems specialist and parametric designer focused on the intersection of architecture, manufacturing, automation, and product development. My professional journey has been driven by a singular interest: understanding how complex physical systems can be designed, refined, and ultimately automated.

My background spans architectural fabrication, computational design, manufacturing systems, rapid prototyping, and process improvement. Throughout my career, I have worked to bridge the gap between design intent and production execution, developing solutions that improve efficiency, reduce complexity, and create more scalable workflows. Whether creating production tooling, developing parametric models, refining manufacturing processes, or implementing operational improvements, my work has centered on transforming challenging problems into practical, repeatable systems.

A significant portion of my experience has involved developing configurable and parametric design tools using platforms such as Onshape and Grasshopper. By leveraging variables, configurations, and computational design methodologies, I create systems that reduce manual effort, standardize decision-making, and enable teams to operate more effectively. These projects have demonstrated how thoughtful design and digital tools can transform specialized knowledge into scalable processes accessible to a broader organization.

Beyond design and modeling, I am deeply interested in systems optimization and mechanical improvement. I enjoy analyzing how people, equipment, and workflows interact, identifying sources of inefficiency, and developing solutions that improve reliability, throughput, maintainability, and overall operational performance. My work has included production fixtures, material handling systems, manufacturing workflow development, machine performance analysis, and continuous improvement initiatives aimed at creating more resilient and efficient operations.

While these experiences have been valuable in their own right, I view them as part of a larger trajectory. Every product, fixture, workflow, and system I develop serves as preparation for the next generation of automated construction and manufacturing technologies. I am particularly interested in the future of modular construction, robotic assembly, digital fabrication, and design-for-manufacturing methodologies that will redefine how buildings and products are created.

My long-term goal is to contribute to the development of highly automated construction systems where computational design, robotics, manufacturing, and architecture converge. I am fascinated by the challenge of creating integrated systems capable of transforming construction from a labor-intensive process into one driven by intelligent automation, repeatability, precision, and data-informed decision making.

Outside of my professional work, I enjoy staying active through tennis, running, and regular fitness training. Physical activity provides an important balance to technical problem-solving and offers an opportunity to continually challenge myself, set goals, and measure progress. I have also developed a growing interest in golf, where I appreciate the combination of strategy, precision, and lifelong learning that the game demands. Whether in sports, fitness, or product development, I am motivated by continuous improvement and the pursuit of mastery through deliberate practice.

I believe the most impactful innovations emerge at the intersection of disciplines. By combining architectural thinking, manufacturing expertise, computational design, systems optimization, and hands-on product development, I aim to help shape a future where automation, robotics, and intelligent design fundamentally transform how we build, manufacture, and interact with the built environment.

Learning Framework

EDUCATION & LEARNING PHILOSOPHY

My professional foundation began in architecture, where I studied design, digital modeling, and the relationship between form, function, and construction.

While my learning started in a traditional academic setting, much of my professional development has been shaped through hands-on experience, self-directed study, and the pursuit of increasingly complex challenges. Working across manufacturing, computational design, product development, and systems optimization has provided opportunities to build practical expertise while continuously expanding my technical capabilities.

I view learning as a lifelong discipline rather than a fixed milestone. Every project presents an opportunity to develop new skills, explore unfamiliar technologies, and deepen my understanding of how design, manufacturing, and automation intersect. This mindset has led me to continually invest in areas such as digital fabrication, robotics, modular construction, systems thinking, and process improvement.

Outside of project work, I maintain an active reading practice focused on engineering, manufacturing, leadership, innovation, continuous improvement, and personal development. The reading list featured throughout this website highlights many of the books and resources that have influenced my thinking and helped shape my approach to problem solving, decision making, and professional growth.

I believe meaningful expertise is built through a combination of education, practical experience, intellectual curiosity, and consistent effort over time. Whether developing a new product, refining a manufacturing process, or exploring emerging technologies, I approach each challenge with a growth mindset and the perspective of a lifelong student.
